## Full description (max 4000)

Simple Video Editor puts the everyday video edits you actually need in one clean, fast app. Cut your clips, add titles and music, pick a look and export — all on your phone, with no account and no watermark.

✂️ TRIM, SPLIT & MERGE
• Trim the start and end of any clip with precise sliders
• Split at the playhead to cut out the parts you don't want
• Combine multiple videos and photos into one video, in any order
• Duplicate, reorder and delete clips, with full undo and redo

⚡ SPEED CONTROL
• Slow motion down to 0.1x or fast-forward up to 8x
• Speed ramps — Montage, Hero, Bullet, Flash in and Flash out — for that cinematic slow-fast effect
• Audio follows the speed so everything stays in sync

🔤 TEXT & CAPTIONS
• Add titles anywhere on screen with your own size, colour and background box
• Animate text with Pop, Slide up, Fade or Typewriter effects
• Add subtitle lines one by one, import .srt or .vtt subtitle files, or paste a script and let the app time the captions for you
• Style one caption and apply it to all of them

🎵 MUSIC & AUDIO
• Add background music that loops under your whole video, with fade in and fade out
• Drop in sound effects exactly where you want them
• Record voiceovers directly in the app
• Adjust the volume of every clip and track, or mute the original sound
• Built-in noise reduction to clean up rumble and background hiss

🎨 FILTERS & COLOUR
• 10 filters: Vivid, Warm, Cool, Cinematic, Vintage, Faded, B&W, Noir, Sepia and Invert
• Fine-tune brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth and vignette
• Apply a look to one clip or to the whole video

🎬 TRANSITIONS & EFFECTS
• Fade to black, white flash, wipe, slide, zoom and circle reveal transitions
• Intro and outro fades
• Green screen (chroma key): remove a green or blue background and replace it with a colour or your own photo

📐 READY FOR EVERY PLATFORM
• Reframe for vertical 9:16 stories and short videos, 1:1 square, 4:5 portrait or 16:9 widescreen
• Fit with bars or fill the frame with a crop
• Export in 720p or 1080p MP4

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your videos never leave your device. All editing and rendering happens on your phone using its hardware video encoder — no uploads, no account, no ads, no watermark.

Save your finished video straight to your gallery or share it to your favourite apps.
