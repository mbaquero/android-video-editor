# Privacy Policy — Simple Video Editor

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

Simple Video Editor ("the app") is free: it has no ads, no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and
needs no account. **The app does not collect, store or share any personal data.**

## What the app accesses

- **Photos and videos** that you choose with the system picker, so you can edit them, and access to save the videos you export to your gallery.
- **Microphone**, only while you record a voiceover from the Audio tool.
- **Audio and subtitle files** that you choose with the system file picker.

## How that data is used

All editing and rendering happens on your device. Your media, recordings and exported videos are
never uploaded, and the app makes no network requests. Temporary files (such as thumbnails and
rendered previews) are kept in the app's private cache, and Android may clear them at any time.

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not require an account.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
